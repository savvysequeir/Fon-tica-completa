II. Explicación

2.1 Mixed Conditional 1

If + past perfect, would/could/might + verbo base.

Une una condición imaginaria del pasado con una consecuencia en el presente.

If I had studied English, I would speak better now.

2.2 Mixed Conditional 2

If + past simple, would/could/might + have + participio pasado.

Une una condición imaginaria presente con una consecuencia imaginaria del pasado.

If I were more careful, I would have avoided the mistake.

2.3 Orden flexible

Las oraciones pueden iniciar con If o con la consecuencia.

If she had practiced, she would be confident now.
She would be confident now if she had practiced.

2.4 Negativas

If she had not arrived late, she would be here now.
If he were not so careless, he would have finished earlier.

2.5 Interrogativas

Would you be ready now if you had studied?
Would they have passed if they were more responsible?

2.6 Clave de uso

Mixed 1: pasado afecta el presente. Mixed 2: presente explica un resultado pasado.
